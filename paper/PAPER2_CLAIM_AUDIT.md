# Paper 2 Claim Audit

## Allowed manuscript claims

- B4 provides higher selected-case reliability than B3 on the frozen and declared supplemental banks.
- The frozen B4 selected-case result is 1468/1468 successes with a one-sided 95% Wilson lower bound of 99.816%.
- B4 has materially lower unconditional joint-QoS probability than B3 in the frozen paired benchmark.
- Robust uncertainty treatment contracts the selected operating region while preserving higher conditional reliability on the tested uncertainty banks.
- The physical feasibility gate removes actions that cannot represent the requested range/radial-velocity state under the declared profile equations.
- Finite-draw calibration quantifies disagreement with a nested 4096-draw comparator; it does not establish physical truth.
- Host-runtime measurements quantify computational cost of the reference Python/Linux implementation.

## Explicitly excluded

- No claim of universal or unconditional B4 superiority.
- No claim of arbitrary-SNR or arbitrary-interference robustness.
- No claim of global Pareto optimality outside realized evaluated operating points.
- No claim of embedded or vehicle-level real-time execution.
- No claim of RF hardware validation or field deployment.
- No claim of standards compliance.
- No claim that the composite high-mobility profile is a commercial preset.
- No claim that the scenario/Wilson/selective-prediction statistical tools are novel in themselves.

## Numerical consistency rule

The canonical runtime values are 32.17, 63.81, 127.19, and 251.04 ms median B4 decision latency for 64, 128, 256, and 512 uncertainty draws, respectively, with a 504.11 ms 95th percentile at 512 draws. Older draft values must not be reintroduced.

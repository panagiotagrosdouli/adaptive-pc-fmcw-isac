# Reviewer Defense

- Novelty is the combination of PC-FMCW physical feasibility gating, finite-action joint communication/sensing adaptation, uncertainty-aware confidence qualification, abstention, and failure-boundary analysis.
- B3 remains an important comparator because it selects more often and has higher unconditional joint QoS in the frozen benchmark, while B4 is more conservative and has stronger selected-case reliability.
- Wilson qualification is used so finite Monte Carlo success counts are not presented as exact probabilities.
- The 512-draw setting is the frozen protocol choice; supplemental calibration shows lower feasibility disagreement with larger draw budgets but nonzero selected-action disagreement remains.
- The 1468/1468 B4 selected successes support a strong conditional result under the frozen simulation protocol, not a field or hardware guarantee.
- Lower B4 availability is expected because abstention is part of the controller objective.
- Robustness statements are restricted to tested uncertainty, distribution-shift, and mismatch families.
- The high-mobility profile is a composite capability reference, not a claimed commercial preset.
- Runtime is host/Python evidence, not ECU certification.
- Oracle is non-deployable and is used only as a hindsight reference.
- Frozen thresholds are predeclared; no post-hoc threshold tuning is allowed by the v2.1 protocol.

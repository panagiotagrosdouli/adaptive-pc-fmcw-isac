# Reference Verification

Date: 2026-09-12

This is the single repository record for bibliography verification. It replaces duplicate audit-marker files removed during the cleanup pass.

## Verification policy

Every citation used to support a manuscript claim must resolve to a real publication or primary technical document, and the manuscript wording must stay within what that source actually establishes. Priority is given to publisher pages, DOI metadata, institutional publication records, government indexes, and vendor primary documentation.

## Claim boundaries

The cited experimental PC-FMCW literature establishes prior waveform/receiver feasibility and related sensing/communication behavior. Those measurements are external prior art and are not measurements produced by this repository. The manuscript therefore continues to describe its own numerical evidence as simulation/analytical only.

No citation audit supports a categorical first-of-kind claim for robust ISAC, adaptive waveform selection, chance-constrained optimization, or PC-FMCW itself. Novelty is deliberately scoped to the paper’s specific composition of deterministic PC-FMCW physical capability gating, finite-action reliability-constrained adaptation, explicit abstention, and confidence-qualified feasible-operating-region characterization.

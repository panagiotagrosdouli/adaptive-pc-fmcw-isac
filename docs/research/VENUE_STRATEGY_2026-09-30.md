# Venue Strategy — 2026-09-30

## Current manuscript profile

- IEEEtran, approximately six pages in the repository audit.
- Simulation/analytical/statistical contribution, not a new RF hardware prototype.
- Core topic: adaptive PC-FMCW / automotive radar / ISAC / uncertainty / high mobility.
- Strongest contribution: physics-gated, confidence-qualified finite-action selection with explicit abstention and reliability--availability--computation characterization.

## Immediate-fit route: IEEE Radar Conference 2027

The 2027 IEEE Radar Conference explicitly lists ISAC and advanced ISAC waveforms in its technical scope.

The current conference website specifies:

- conference: 1--6 May 2027, Bangalore, India;
- full paper: IEEE two-column format;
- length: 3--6 pages including figures, tables, and references;
- current published submission deadline: 31 October 2026.

This is structurally close to the current six-page manuscript and the paper's radar/ISAC emphasis.

### What must change for RadarConf

- Switch from journal to IEEE conference template.
- Compress/verify the paper at a strict six-page maximum.
- Replace anonymous author metadata if the submission is not blind.
- Keep the physics-gate and operating-region story central.
- Treat the deep literature audit as internal provenance; do not overload the paper with every audited reference.
- Verify the conference's final PDF and copyright requirements before upload.

## Alternative conference route: Radar 2027 (IET)

The IET International Conference on Radar Systems 2027 currently lists:

- full paper deadline: 22 February 2027;
- conference papers generally recommended at five--six pages;
- publication on the IET Digital Library and IEEE Xplore for successful papers;
- broad radar technologies, signal processing, and applications scope.

This offers more preparation time but is less explicitly ISAC-centered than IEEE RadarConf.

## JC&S 2027

The 7th IEEE International Symposium on Joint Communications & Sensing is scheduled for 25--29 January 2027 in Munich.

Its scope is directly aligned with joint communications and sensing, including system and implementation aspects. As of the 2026-09-30 audit, the public site states that technical-paper deadlines are still to be announced.

This is thematically excellent, but the submission calendar must be checked again before treating it as the primary route.

## Journal route: IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology

TVT is a strong thematic fit because its scope covers electrical/electronic technology in vehicles, vehicular wireless communications, V2X, interference, cognitive communications, and command/control for wireless systems.

Current author instructions allow up to 14 double-column pages for an initial regular-paper submission.

### What a TVT version should add

The current six-page paper is better viewed as a compact conference-grade story. A journal version should use the additional space to strengthen at least some of:

- higher-fidelity mobility/channel modeling;
- oscillator/synchronization uncertainty model;
- larger and more diverse action spaces;
- stronger theoretical treatment of the confidence/abstention rule;
- hardware-in-the-loop or RF measurements if feasible;
- computational acceleration and real-time feasibility;
- broader comparison with adaptive FMCW/ISAC baselines.

A journal submission should not simply stretch the present six-page text.

## IEEE Transactions on Communications

TCOM accepts original theoretical and/or experimental communications work of permanent reference value. The 2026 Temiz et al. FMCW ISAC paper is already published there, which makes TCOM scientifically relevant but also raises the comparison bar.

For a TCOM submission, the present work would benefit from a stronger theoretical reliability formulation and/or hardware/HIL evidence beyond the current simulation operating-region study.

## Practical recommendation

For the **current evidence package**, prepare a conference submission first, with IEEE RadarConf 2027 as a particularly natural fit if the submission schedule and attendance requirements are practical.

For a subsequent journal extension, TVT is a natural direction if the study is expanded rather than merely lengthened.

This strategy is a venue-fit recommendation, not an acceptance prediction.

# Literature Positioning Audit

Date: 2026-09-12

The literature audit supports a conservative positioning of the contribution.

Established prior art includes PC-FMCW sensing/communication demonstrations, receiver structures, code-dependent sensing behavior, smoothed PC-FMCW transceiver design, coherent MIMO PC-FMCW, automotive-radar interference mitigation, broad ISAC waveform/co-design research, and chance/distributionally robust resource allocation under uncertain vehicular CSI.

Accordingly, this manuscript does **not** claim invention of PC-FMCW, phase coding, generic ISAC, generic robust/chance-constrained optimization, generic adaptive waveform selection, MIMO coding, or radar interference mitigation.

The manuscript's defensible distinction is the ordered composition and evaluation of a PC-FMCW-specific hard physical capability gate with reliability-constrained finite-action PHY selection under uncertain sensing/communication state, together with explicit separation of physical infeasibility, controller abstention, conditional reliability, unconditional availability, and computational cost.

The literature audit did not establish categorical priority for this exact composition. The appropriate novelty language is therefore comparative (for example, "comparatively underexplored" or "to the best of our review") rather than "first" or "unique".

This conservative positioning is intentional: it improves reviewer defensibility without weakening the actual computational contribution.

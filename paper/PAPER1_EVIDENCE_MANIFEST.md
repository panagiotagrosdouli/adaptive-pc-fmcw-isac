## Gate-ablation interpretation boundary

The Paper 1 ablation compares deterministic nominal selectors that use the same QoS screen and resource ranking; the only intended difference is whether canonical physical-feasibility filtering occurs before ranking. The boundary-aware grid is a capability stress grid, not an empirical distribution of real driving states. Therefore its invalid-selection fraction must not be presented as a field prevalence estimate.

## Claim boundary

No item in this manifest is a hardware measurement. The high-mobility profile is not claimed to be a commercial preset. The pilot evidence is not substituted for the later frozen large-seed reliability benchmark. Paper 2 Wilson qualification, B4 reliability evidence, and reliability--availability--computation claims are not used as Paper 1 evidence.
